PILLAR III // AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS

Coming soon

Machines that speak the region.

Drones and robots are learning to see. We are teaching them to read, listen, and speak Farsi, Pashto, Urdu, Russian, and the region’s other languages, and to understand its culture.

01 // THE LANGUAGE LAYER

The platform comes later. The language starts now.

An unmanned system in Southwest or Central Asia meets spoken commands, street signs, documents, and people. Without the language layer, it sees everything and understands nothing.

We focus on that layer for drones (UAS) and ground robots (UGVs): what they read, what they hear, and what they say. Building platforms of our own is a later step.
