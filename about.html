ABOUT // B-COGNITIVE SYSTEMS

Built on the honeybee.

A defense and intelligence firm founded on reconnaissance, precise communication, and collective defense.

01 // FOUNDER & CEO

Experience where language meets policy.

Ahmad Shakibi founded B-Cognitive Systems on a conviction shaped by sixteen years of language work: that understanding a region begins with understanding its words. A language analyst and subject-matter expert in counterterrorism and nonproliferation, he has built his career where language, diplomacy, and security meet, including years supporting US government engagement in Afghanistan.

He has interpreted for ambassadors, diplomats, attachés, and senior military officials, carrying meaning faithfully across languages in rooms where precision mattered. Alongside that work, he analyzed sensitive political and security documents and assessed the local dynamics behind them.

His counterterrorism expertise centers on the Af-Pak region and the armed groups operating across Afghanistan, Pakistan, and Central Asia. In nonproliferation, he specializes in Iran’s nuclear weapons program, its missile and drone capability, and its network of active proxies.

Looking ahead, he brings experience in cyber threat intelligence and is advancing research into autonomous systems, exploring how language intelligence can give smart and unmanned platforms the understanding they need to operate across the region.

As founder and CEO, he leads B-Cognitive Systems’ analysis, client engagement, and growing network of vetted linguists and analysts.

02 // MISSION & VISION

Why we exist.

MISSION

B-Cognitive Systems delivers decision advantage by reading Southwest and Central Asia in its own languages. We turn native-language sources into intelligence, apply that understanding to cyber threats, and give autonomous systems the language and cultural understanding to operate in the region.

VISION

A future where no mission fails for lack of understanding, where analysts, cyber defenders, and the machines that support them comprehend the region as fluently as the people who live there.

03 // WHAT INSPIRED US

The honeybee.

A bee leaves the hive, scouts unknown terrain, and returns with intelligence. Through a precise dance, it tells the colony exactly where to go: the angle encodes direction, the duration encodes distance, and the vigor encodes value.

The colony acts on that report without ever seeing the target, so the message must be exact. One wrong degree, and the mission fails. We built B-Cognitive Systems on the same principles: disciplined reconnaissance, precise communication, and collective defense. In our work, the dance is language, and getting it exactly right is the difference between understanding and misreading the region.

CORE SPECIALIZATION
