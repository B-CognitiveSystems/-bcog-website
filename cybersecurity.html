Threats speak a language. We read it.

Cyber threat intelligence that starts where most analysts can’t: in the Farsi, Russian, Pashto, and Urdu that threat actors actually use. Official term: native-language cyber threat intelligence (CTI) supporting attribution.

01 // WHAT WE MEAN

Cyber-linguistic intelligence, defined.

Cyber threat intelligence (CTI) explains who is attacking, why, and what comes next. Much of the evidence is written in the attacker’s language: claims of responsibility, recruitment posts, lure documents, and strings left in code.

Machine translation flattens idiom, dialect, and tone. Language analysis recovers them, and with them, intent, origin, and credibility. That is where our language expertise solves problems other CTI teams can’t.
